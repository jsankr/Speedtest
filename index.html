DNS speed benchmark

DNS turns names like google.com into addresses before every page loads. This compares five public DNS services from your connection, then tells you exactly what to enter in your router.

Contacts each DNS service directly over encrypted DNS (DNS-over-HTTPS). "Cached" is a popular name the service already knows; "fresh" is a random name it must look up from scratch.

Device maker lookup

Paste MAC addresses from your router's connected-devices list to see who made each device. Unknown makers are worth blocking.

Looked up on this device from the IEEE maker registry built into CleanSpeed; addresses are never sent anywhere. Registry data: oui-data by silverwind (BSD-2-Clause), from IEEE.

Subnet & IP calculator

Tip: enter your router's WAN or Internet IP from its Status page. An address from 100.64.0.0 to 100.127.255.255 means your provider uses carrier-grade NAT.

Not possible in any web page, so not included: traceroute, port scanning, Wi-Fi signal and channel scanning, and finding devices on your network. Browsers block these for security; use your router's own app or a desktop tool such as nmap.

Router security check

This checks your router's model and firmware against the U.S. National Vulnerability Database (NVD), flags issues being actively exploited, and grades your settings. Web browsers don't let any website log into your router or scan your home network, so you enter the details once; they stay on this device.

1 · Your router
Where do I find these?
  • Label under the router: brand, model and hardware version (often "Ver 3.0" or "V3").
  • Firmware version: log in to the router's admin page, then look under Status, System or Firmware Upgrade.
  • Admin page address: usually printed on the label. Common ones: 192.168.0.1, 192.168.1.1, 192.168.29.1 (JioFiber), 192.168.1.254.

    Prefix base

    1 byte = 8 bits, so 1 MB/s = 8 Mbps and 1 GB/s = 8 Gbps. Internet plans are sold in bits (Mbps/Gbps); file downloads show bytes (MB/s). Each test moves roughly your speed × twice the phase length, so lower the seconds per phase on mobile data.
